7.1. Countries

Conventional rules apply to the designations, abbreviations and listing order of Member States, candidate countries and non-EU countries.

Conventional rules apply to the designations, abbreviations and listing order of Member States, candidate countries and non-EU countries.

  • 7.1.1. Designations and abbreviations to use
    The abbreviations to use for Member States and non-EU countries are mainly the ISO codes and the designation to use (long or short form) depends on the context.
  • 7.1.2. Country listing order
    The order of listing differs for Member States and non-EU countries and also in text and tables.

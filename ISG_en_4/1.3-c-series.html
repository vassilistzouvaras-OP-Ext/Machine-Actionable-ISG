1.3. C series

The C series covers a wide variety of documents grouped by headings and subheadings.

The C series covers a wide variety of documents grouped by headings and subheadings.

  • 1.3.1. Classification of documents
    Documents published in the C series are classified under five headings: Resolutions, recommendations and opinions (C I), Information (C II), Preparatory acts (C III), Notices (C IV), and Announcements (C V).
  • 1.3.2. Numbering of documents
    With the exception of corrigenda, documents published in the C series are assigned a notice number by the Publications Office.

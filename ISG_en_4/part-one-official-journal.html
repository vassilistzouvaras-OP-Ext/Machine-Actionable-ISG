Part One Official Journal

The Official Journal of the European Union, the documents published in it and specific drafting rules are described here.

The Official Journal of the European Union, the documents published in it and specific drafting rules are described here. It should be read together with the conventions common to all languages, the language-specific rules and the annexes.

  • Preamble
    The preamble to the Official Journal part contains an overview of its history, of author services and of tools and reference works used for texts to be published in it.
  • 1. Structure of the Official Journal
    EU legal acts and documents are published in different series of the Official Journal.
  • 2. Structure of a legal act
    Legal acts follow a strict structure and – depending on the complexity of the text – parts, titles, chapters or sections may be used in the preamble, enacting terms and annexes.
  • 3. Rules on drafting documents
    Texts for publication in the Official Journal must exist in each of the official languages of the EU and follow strict drafting rules.
  • Summary tables
    These tables summarise the content and numbering of the Official Journal and the distinctive elements and numbering of different types of acts, and are followed by model that shows the structure of an act.

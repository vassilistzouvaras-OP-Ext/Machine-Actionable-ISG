7.3. Currencies

There are conventional rules for the use of the euro and other currencies concerning their listing order, the different ways to write them, the punctuation to use, etc.

There are conventional rules for the use of the euro and other currencies concerning their listing order, the different ways to write them, the punctuation to use, etc.

  • 7.3.1. The euro and the cent
    The euro is the currency used in most of the European Union; its subunit is the cent.
  • 7.3.2. Order of the currencies and ISO codes
    When using currency abbreviations, the ISO codes 4217 should be used.
  • 7.3.3. Rules for expressing monetary units
    Monetary units can be expressed by using their name, their ISO code or their sign.

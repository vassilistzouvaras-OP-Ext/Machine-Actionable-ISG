1. Structure of the Official Journal

EU legal acts and documents are published in different series of the Official Journal.

EU legal acts and documents are published in different series of the Official Journal.

  • 1.1. General structure
    The Official Journal consists of three series: Legislation (L), Information and Notices (C), and Supplement to the Official Journal of the European Union (S).
  • 1.2. L series
    The L series covers legislative, non-legislative and other acts grouped by headings and subheadings.
  • 1.3. C series
    The C series covers a wide variety of documents grouped by headings and subheadings.

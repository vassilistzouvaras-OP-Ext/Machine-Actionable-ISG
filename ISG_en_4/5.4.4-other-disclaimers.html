  3. This report does not involve the [institution/body/office/agency] in liability of any kind.

  4. The contents of this publication do not necessarily reflect the position or opinion of the [institution/body/office/agency].

  5. The opinions expressed are those of the author(s) only and should not be considered as representative of the [institution/body/office/agency]’s official position.

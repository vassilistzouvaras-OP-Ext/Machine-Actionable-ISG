3.3. Amending provisions

There are provisions concerning the amendments and the numbering in the amended act.

There are provisions concerning the amendments and the numbering in the amended act.

  • 3.3.1. Amendments in the text
    Amendments in the text are introduced and presented according to whether they relate to a full article, a marked or an unmarked subdivision, a sentence or part of a sentence.
  • 3.3.2. Additions and numbering
    Where articles, numbered paragraphs or other subdivisions that are identified by a number or a letter (marked subdivisions) are inserted into the enacting terms of an existing act, specific numbering rules apply.

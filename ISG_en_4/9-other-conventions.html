9. Other conventions

Other conventions concern addresses, electronic addresses, telephone numbers, the citation of works, the administrative structure of the European Union, etc.

Other conventions concern addresses, electronic addresses, telephone numbers, the citation of works, the administrative structure of the European Union, etc.

  • 9.1. Addresses
    In principle, the international format is always used in the works of the European Union institutions; the language to use depends on whether the work is unilingual or multilingual.
  • 9.2.Electronic addresses
    Electronic addresses are introduced by the term ‘Email’ or ‘Internet’, followed by a colon.
  • 9.3. Telephone numbers
    For reasons of simplification, the EU authorities have agreed on a uniform presentation in all EU languages.
  • 9.4. Citation of works and references to the Official Journal
    Citations of works in the bibliography must all list the components in the same order and have the same presentation, which is identical in all the language versions.
  • 9.5. Administrative structure of the European Union: official titles and listing order
    The European Union’s administrative structure consists of institutions and bodies, interinstitutional services, agencies and other organisations
  • 9.6. Commission directorates-general and services: official titles
    Commission directorates-general and services have full and short names, commonly used abbreviations, internal codes and fields.

The specific rules and conventions of the English language, together with a list of reference works, complement the common conventions.

  • 10. House rules for the preparation of the text
    House rules – approved by the interinstitutional linguistic group – contain information on punctuation, verb usage, spelling, upper and lower case, numbers, dates, time, etc.

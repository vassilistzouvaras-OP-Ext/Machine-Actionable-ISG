4.1. Authors, authorising departments, Publications Office and printshops

Authors, project managers and proofreaders participate in the production process of a text.

Authors, project managers and proofreaders participate in the production process of a text.

  • 4.1.1. Authors and the Publications Office
    The services provided by the Publications Office to authors include preparation of publications in various formats, graphic design, proofreading, printing on demand and provision of identifiers.
  • 4.1.2. Project management at the Publications Office
    The project managers’ role is to carry out the typographical preparation of the manuscript, and the graphic-design office can also produce drafts and models for some projects.
  • 4.1.3. Proofreading at the Publications Office
    Proofreaders, who cast a fresh eye over the text to be published, are responsible for checking both linguistic matters (language rules and conventions) and technical aspects (typographical conventions).

5.1. Cover

The cover of a book consists of four pages referred to as the front cover, the inside front cover, the inside back cover and the back cover.

The cover of a book consists of four pages referred to as the front cover, the inside front cover, the inside back cover and the back cover. If the book is thick enough the title should be printed on the spine, reading downwards. The type size used must be in proportion to the width of the spine.

  • 5.1.1. Components
    Each of the four cover pages has distinctive components.
  • 5.1.2. Use of colour on covers
    The use of colour on covers should be confined to the graphic design in general, and to distinguishing collections and series.

10.3. Spelling

A number of spelling conventions, tricky plurals and interference effects are highlighted here.

A number of spelling conventions, tricky plurals and interference effects are highlighted here.

  • 10.3.1. Conventions
    Regarding English spelling, the standard usage of Ireland and the United Kingdom is to be followed.
  • 10.3.2. Tricky plurals
    Some nouns do not form the plural by adding an -s to the end of the word.
  • 10.3.3. Interference effects and in-house words, expressions and constructions
    Especially in texts intended for the general public, care should be taken to avoid interference effects and the use of in-house words, expressions, constructions and meanings that are not generally recognised outside the EU institutions.

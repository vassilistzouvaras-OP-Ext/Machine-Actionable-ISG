2.2. Preamble

Preamble means everything between the title and the enacting terms of the act.

Preamble means everything between the title and the enacting terms of the act.

  • 2.2.1. Citations
    Citations indicate the legal basis for the act and, where applicable, the preparatory acts, the transmission of the draft legislative act to national parliaments and the procedure followed.
  • 2.2.2. Recitals
    Recitals set out the reasons for the contents of the enacting terms (i.e. the articles) of an act.

3.4.5. Currencies

Names and abbreviations of currencies and the order in which they appear are defined to be used in the same way in all EU texts.

For the names and abbreviations of currencies and the order in which they appear, see Section 7.3 and Annex A7.

For the rules for expressing monetary units, see Section 7.3.3.

5.3. Verso of the title page

The verso of the title page has distinctive components.

The verso of the title page has distinctive components.

  • 5.3.1. Components
    The components on the verso of the title page always include the place of publication, the name of the publisher, the year of publication, copyright details and identifiers.
  • 5.3.2. Information relating to the printed paper product
    Any printed paper product must in principle include the name of the printer and the country of printing, the applicable ecolabel(s) and possibly a note relating to the paper used.

10.2. Verbs

Verbs describe an action, a process, or a state of being, and are used together with a noun or pronoun.

Verbs describe an action, a process, or a state of being, and are used together with a noun or pronoun.

  • 10.2.1. Singular or plural agreement?
    There are specific rules for collective nouns, sums of money, percentages or fractions, countries and organisations with plural names, etc.
  • 10.2.2. Tenses of minutes and summary records
    Minutes and summary records are written as reported speech following the sequence of tenses.

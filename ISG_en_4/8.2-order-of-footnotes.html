8.2. Order of footnotes

The order of footnotes and other notes is the same in all language versions.

In principle, the order of footnotes is the same in all language versions.

In tables, graphs, etc., the different types of notes are presented in a certain order. The footnotes marked with asterisks are listed first, followed by the numbered footnotes. They may be followed by an NB or source(s), separated from the footnotes by a slightly larger space than the one between the footnotes:

(*)
Interim Commission decision.
(1)
These prices are the result of applying a maximum reduction.
(2)
For the current marketing year, this price is increased by a special premium.

NB: Provisional figures.

Source: European Commission, DG Agriculture and Rural Development.

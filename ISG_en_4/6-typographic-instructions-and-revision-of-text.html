6. Typographic instructions and revision of the text

Working conventions regarding typographic instructions and standardised presentation are followed to prepare harmonised publications in a multilingual environment.

Working conventions regarding typographic instructions and standardised presentation are followed to prepare harmonised publications in a multilingual environment.

  • 6.1. Reading the manuscript
    Proofreaders of the Publications Office read through the manuscript to have an overall view of the work and notify the author services in case of problems.
  • 6.2. Reading electronic manuscripts
    Specific programs can be used for checking the structure of the text and the correct use and application of the house style.
  • 6.3. Proof-correction marks
    The first proof is compared against the manuscript and any necessary corrections are indicated with proof-correction marks.
  • 6.4. Word-processor punctuation marks and spacing
    Punctuation marks and spacing are based on interinstitutional agreements, on national typographic codes and on established common multilingual standards.
  • 6.5. Punctuation in figures
    A point is used to separate whole numbers from decimals, except in the Official Journal, and a space is used to separate thousands in whole numbers.6.5

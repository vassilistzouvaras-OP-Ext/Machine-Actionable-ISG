7. Countries, languages, currencies and their abbreviations

Common conventions simplify and harmonise the writing of country names, languages, currencies and their abbreviations, along with their listing order in different cases.

Common conventions simplify and harmonise the writing of country names, languages, currencies and their abbreviations, along with their listing order in different cases.

  • 7.1. Countries
    Conventional rules apply to the designations, abbreviations and listing order of Member States, candidate countries and non-EU countries.
  • 7.2. Languages
    Different rules apply when listing language versions and when listing languages.
  • 7.3. Currencies
    There are conventional rules for the use of the euro and other currencies concerning their listing order, the different ways to write them, the punctuation to use, etc.

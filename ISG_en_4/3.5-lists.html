3.5. Lists

Listed points and acts listed in a text follow specific rules.

Listed points and acts listed in a text follow specific rules.

  • 3.5.1. Listed points – presentation
    Listed points are usually preceded by an introductory phrase, and the numbering and punctuation to be used depend on the level.
  • 3.5.2. Listing of acts
    When listing acts of the same type, neither the name of the author nor the type of act is repeated if it is identical.

9.5. Administrative structure of the European Union: official titles and listing order

The European Union’s administrative structure consists of institutions and bodies, interinstitutional services, agencies and other organisations

The European Union’s administrative structure consists of institutions and bodies, interinstitutional services, agencies and other organisations.

tip:

See also Annex A9 – Institutions, bodies, offices and agencies: multilingual list

  • 9.5.1. Institutions and bodies
    The institutions and bodies must be listed in protocol order.
  • 9.5.2. Interinstitutional services
    The interinstitutional services are attached administratively to the European Commission.
  • 9.5.3. Decentralised agencies
    Decentralised agencies are created by a specific legislative act and charged with a particular task.
  • 9.5.4. Common foreign and security policy agencies and bodies
    These agencies and bodies are established by the Council within the context of the common foreign and security policy (CFSP).
  • 9.5.5. Executive agencies
    Executive agencies are legal entities set up for a fixed period with a view to being entrusted with certain tasks.
  • 9.5.6. Euratom agencies and bodies
    These agencies or bodies have been created to support the aims of the European Atomic Energy Community Treaty (Euratom).
  • 9.5.7. Other organisations
    The other organisations listed here include joint undertakings, set up for cooperation in EU research, technological development and demonstration programmes.

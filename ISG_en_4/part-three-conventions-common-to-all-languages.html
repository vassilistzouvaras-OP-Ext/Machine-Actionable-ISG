Part Three Conventions common to all languages

Common conventions are linguistic rules that the European Union institutions have agreed on to ensure harmonisation in the 24 official EU languages.

Common conventions are linguistic rules that the European Union institutions have agreed on to ensure harmonisation in the 24 official EU languages.

  • 6. Typographic instructions and revision of the text
    Working conventions regarding typographic instructions and standardised presentation are followed to prepare harmonised publications in a multilingual environment.
  • 7. Countries, languages, currencies and their abbreviations
    Common conventions simplify and harmonise the writing of country names, languages, currencies and their abbreviations, along with their listing order in different cases.
  • 8. Footnotes and their references
    Footnotes are normally placed at the foot of the corresponding page.
  • 9. Other conventions
    Other conventions concern addresses, electronic addresses, telephone numbers, the citation of works, the administrative structure of the European Union, etc.

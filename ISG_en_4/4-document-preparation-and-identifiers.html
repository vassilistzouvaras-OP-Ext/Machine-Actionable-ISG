4. Document preparation and identifiers

Documents are prepared by various actors during the publication process, and identifiers are assigned depending on the types of publication.

Documents are prepared by various actors during the publication process, and identifiers are assigned depending on the types of publication.

  • 4.1. Authors, authorising departments, Publications Office and printshops
    Authors, project managers and proofreaders participate in the production process of a text.
  • 4.2. Original documents (manuscripts)
    Manuscript text preparation and word processing are linked to various IT procedures that are recommended to optimise the preparation of texts for publication.
  • 4.3. Publication types
    Document identification and classification is governed at international level by different agreements and standards.
  • 4.4. Identifiers assigned by the Publications Office
    The Publications Office assigns identifiers (ISBN, ISSN, DOI and catalogue number) to publications issued by European Union institutions, bodies, offices and agencies.
  • 4.5. Identifier assigned by the Court of Justice of the European Union
    The European case-law identifier (ECLI) has been developed to facilitate the correct and unequivocal citation of decisions from European and national courts.

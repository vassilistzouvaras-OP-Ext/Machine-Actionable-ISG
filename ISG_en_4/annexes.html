Annexes

The annexes provide, among other things, verified and validated reference data collected together and presented in the form of lists and tables.

The annexes provide, among other things, verified and validated reference data collected together and presented in the form of lists and tables.

  • Annex A1 – Graphics guide to the European emblem
    The graphics guide presents the history of the European flag, gives a technical description of its components and explains how it can be used.
  • Annex A2 – Emblems
    The emblems are reproduced here only for information and without prejudice to the specific conditions fixed by each institution, body, office or agency for their use in reproduction.
  • Annex A3 – Abbreviations and symbols in common use
    The most frequently used civil titles, measurement units, statistical symbols and abbreviations are presented here.
  • Annex A4 – Main acronyms and initialisms
    The list presents the most common acronyms and initialisms, the spelling of which follows specific rules agreed by the institutions.
  • Annex A5 – List of countries, territories and currencies
    The table lists the countries and territories of the world with their short name and full name, the country code, the capital, the demonym, the adjective and the currency.
  • Annex A6 – Country and territory codes
    The names of the countries and territories of the world are listed in the alphabetical order of their two-letter country code (ISO 3166 alpha-2 code).
  • Annex A7 – Currency codes
    This annex contains a table of currencies listed in the alphabetical order of the currency code (ISO 4217), a table listing the candidate-country currencies and a table listing former EU currencies.
  • Annex A8 – Language codes (European Union)
    The tables present the languages of the Member States of the EU, in alphabetical order of languages and in alphabetical order of codes.
  • Annex A9 – Institutions, bodies, offices and agencies: multilingual list
    The list provides a multilingual presentation of the names, in the 24 official EU languages, of each institution, body, office and agency of the European Union.
  • Annex A10 – Regions
    The list shows translations into English of the names of regions in the Member States, based on levels 1 and 2 of the NUTS 2021 classification.
  • Annex C – Miscellaneous
    The table lists specific points, in alphabetical order, which are either problematic or subject to a particular convention.

5.5. Preliminary pages and end matter

The preliminary pages (also called the front matter) include the dedication, foreword, preface and introduction, while the index is part of the end matter.

The preliminary pages (also called the front matter) include the dedication, foreword, preface and introduction, while the index is part of the end matter.

  • 5.5.1. Dedication
    The dedication is generally very short and is printed in characters smaller than those used for the rest of the work.
  • 5.5.2. Foreword, preface and introduction
    The foreword, preface and introduction are distinct parts of the preliminary texts of a work.
  • 5.5.3. Contents
    The contents (headed ‘Contents’) is a table made up of the exact headings that form the sections of the work.
  • 5.5.4. Bibliographies
    Bibliographies are usually placed at the end of a work.
  • 5.5.5. Index
    An index is a detailed list of subjects, persons, places, events, etc. mentioned in a publication, indicating their exact position in the text.
